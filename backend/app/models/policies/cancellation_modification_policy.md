# ThreadNest Order Cancellation and Modification Policy

## 1. Order Cancellation Window and Eligibility
ThreadNest enables customers to modify or cancel their orders conveniently with complete transparency:
- Pre-Dispatch Cancellation: Customers may cancel an order free of charge at any moment prior to dispatch. Eligible order statuses for cancellation include 'pending', 'confirmed', and 'processing'. During these initial fulfillment stages, orders can be cancelled with one click through the customer dashboard or by instructing Tara, the AI Support Assistant.
- Post-Dispatch Cancellation Cutoff: Once an order moves into 'dispatched' status and a carrier tracking Air Waybill (AWB) has been assigned, cancellation is strictly blocked in our order management system. At this stage, physical custody of the parcel has been transferred to third-party courier partners (BlueDart, Delhivery, FedEx) and packages cannot be intercepted or pulled back mid-transit.
- Cancellation Fees: There are zero cancellation charges or penalty fees for any order cancelled prior to dispatch. 100% of the purchase amount, including any paid shipping charges, is refunded to the customer.

## 2. Modifying Delivery Address and Contact Details
ThreadNest understands that delivery details sometimes need urgent adjustment after checkout:
- Pre-Dispatch Address Modification: Customers may update the recipient name, street address, flat/building number, landmark, and contact phone number while the order is in 'pending', 'confirmed', or 'processing' status. The AI Assistant Tara can execute this modification instantly using the update_address tool.
- Pincode and City Alterations: Changes within the same city or local pincode are updated immediately. If updating to a different city or state, the new pincode must be verified for carrier serviceability. If the new destination affects shipping fees or taxation, our customer care desk will facilitate the adjustment.
- Post-Dispatch Address Freeze: Once an order has reached 'dispatched' status, address modifications are technically locked and cannot be edited by support agents. Customers are advised to contact the assigned courier delivery agent directly when they receive the morning out-for-delivery call to provide local landmark guidance.

## 3. Order Item, Size, and Color Modifications Before Dispatch
Customers who wish to alter specific items within an order must follow specific fulfillment procedures:
- Size and Color Adjustments: Because our automated warehouse system reserves inventory and generates picking slips immediately upon order confirmation, individual item parameters (such as changing a T-shirt from Medium to Large, or changing a Kurta color) cannot be edited directly on an active order.
- Recommended Cancellation and Re-Order: The fastest and most reliable method to change apparel size, color, or quantity is to immediately cancel the existing order while it remains in 'pending' or 'processing' status, and place a fresh order with the correct size and item selection.
- Adding Items to an Existing Order: New items cannot be appended to an already confirmed order because invoices, GST records, and package dimensions are generated upon order placement. Customers are encouraged to place a new order for additional items; if placed on the same day, customer support can link both orders to deliver together when feasible.

## 4. Post-Dispatch Procedures and Doorstep Refusal
If an order has already been dispatched and the customer no longer desires the merchandise, the following protocols apply:
- Doorstep Refusal Option: When the courier partner arrives at the delivery address, the customer or recipient has the right to refuse acceptance of the parcel. The customer should inform the delivery driver: 'I wish to cancel and refuse this delivery.'
- Return to Origin (RTO) Movement: The courier will record the package as 'Delivery Refused by Consignee' and initiate reverse transit back to the ThreadNest central fulfillment center.
- Refund Processing on RTO Arrival: For prepaid orders that are refused at delivery, a refund will be initiated once the returned parcel is received and inspected at our warehouse (typically within 5 to 7 business days from refusal).
- Misuse Warning: Repeated doorstep delivery refusals on Cash on Delivery (COD) orders without valid justification may result in temporary or permanent restriction of COD payment privileges for that customer profile.

## 5. Cancellation Refund Timelines and Store Credit Settlement
When an order is successfully cancelled prior to dispatch, refund settlement is processed promptly:
- Prepaid Orders (UPI, Credit/Debit Cards, Net Banking): Cancellation refunds are initiated immediately by our automated system. For UPI transactions (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), refunds typically credit to the customer's linked bank account within 24 to 48 hours. For credit cards, debit cards, and net banking, the funds will reflect within 3 to 5 business days, depending upon the clearing cycle of the issuing banking institution.
- Instant ThreadNest Store Credit: Customers may opt for immediate ThreadNest Store Credit instead of a payment gateway reversal. Store credit is deposited into the customer's account instantly with zero waiting time, never expires, and can be applied toward any future apparel purchase.
- Promo Codes and Vouchers: If a single-use discount coupon code was redeemed on a cancelled order, the coupon code will automatically be reactivated and made available in the customer's account within 2 hours of cancellation.
