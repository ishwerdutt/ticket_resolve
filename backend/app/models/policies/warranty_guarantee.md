# ThreadNest Quality Guarantee and Warranty Policy

## 1. Six-Month Quality Guarantee Scope and Applicability
ThreadNest takes immense pride in the design, craftsmanship, premium textiles, and durability of our entire clothing collection. We stand firmly behind every garment that leaves our manufacturing facilities:
- Guarantee Duration: All apparel purchased directly from ThreadNest is backed by our comprehensive 6-Month Quality Guarantee, commencing from the official date of customer delivery (`delivered_at`).
- Product Range Covered: This quality guarantee covers all primary ThreadNest apparel lines, including Classic Cotton T-shirts, Premium Denim Jeans, Ethnic Kurtas and Kurtis, Casual and Evening Dresses, Winter Hoodies and Sweatshirts, and Traditional Sarees.
- Our Quality Promise: If an eligible garment exhibits manufacturing defects, structural assembly failure, premature fabric breakdown, or hardware malfunctions under standard, normal domestic apparel usage during the 6-month period, ThreadNest will repair the garment, provide a brand-new replacement, or issue full store credit at zero expense to the customer.
- Proof of Purchase: The quality guarantee is linked to your digital customer profile and original order ID, eliminating the need to preserve paper warranty cards or physical retail cash receipts.

## 2. Covered Stitching, Seam, and Hardware Defects
Our 6-Month Quality Guarantee provides robust coverage against specific workmanship flaws, structural failures, and manufacturing errors:
- Stitching and Seam Failure: Premature unravelling of hem stitches, split side seams under regular tension, broken interlock chain stitches, dropped waistline stitches, loose flatlock stitching on activewear, or popped bar-tack reinforcements on pocket corners.
- Hardware and Fasteners: Jammed, detached, or non-functional metallic zippers, broken zipper slider pulls, snapped waistband rivet buttons, defective metal snap buttons, and loose brass rivets on denim jeans.
- Fabric Structural Defects: Unexpected fabric tearing or splitting along seam stress points not caused by external cuts, severe yarn slippage, abnormal fabric skewing/twisting after first wash, and excessive surface pilling that significantly exceeds standard textile tolerances.
- Embellishments and Prints: Excessive peeling, flaking, or delaminating of screen prints, vinyl heat-transfers, or embroidered appliques occurring despite following care instructions.

## 3. Warranty Exclusions and Void Conditions
The quality guarantee is intended to safeguard against manufacturing deficiencies, not damage arising from misuse or accidents. Exclusions include:
- Ordinary Wear and Tear: Natural softening of cotton fibers, minor fading of dark indigo denim or reactive dyed fabrics over dozens of laundry cycles, and expected surface wear from prolonged usage.
- Improper Laundering and Care: Garment damage resulting from failure to adhere to the garment care label attached to the inner seam. This includes using chlorine bleach, caustic detergents, hot iron application directly on rubberized prints, dry-cleaning handloom cottons marked for gentle hand wash, or aggressive high-heat tumble drying.
- External Physical Damage: Snags, cuts, punctures from sharp objects, pet claw tears, burns from cigarette ash or motorcycle exhausts, and stains caused by food, oil, chemical cosmetics, or perfumes.
- Third-Party Modifications: Any garment that has been altered, resized, hemmed, re-dyed, or repaired by an external neighborhood tailor or third-party service automatically voids the warranty.

## 4. Remedy Options: Free Replacement vs Store Credit
When a warranty claim is validated by our Quality Assurance department, ThreadNest offers generous and fair remedies:
- Primary Remedy (Free Brand-New Replacement): ThreadNest will dispatch an identical, brand-new replacement item (same product name, SKU, color, and size) with priority courier delivery at zero charge.
- Secondary Remedy (Store Credit / Refund): If the original item has been discontinued, sold out, or is out of seasonal production, the customer will be issued a 100% store credit voucher or full refund to their original payment source, equal to the purchase price paid for the defective garment. Store credit is valid for 12 months with no minimum spend requirements.
- Zero Cost Guarantee: ThreadNest covers all logistics expenses associated with warranty resolutions. We arrange free reverse pickup of the defective item from the customer's doorstep; customers never pay for reverse transit or replacement shipping.
- Customer Choice Transparency: If an item experiences recurring defects, customers may choose store credit rather than a replacement to pick any other style from our catalogue.

## 5. Claim Submission Process and Staff Verification Workflow
Filing a quality guarantee claim is straightforward and managed through our digital support portal:
- Initiating the Claim: Customers can initiate a warranty claim by contacting Tara, our AI Assistant, or by creating a support ticket using create_ticket with the relevant order ID, item name, and a detailed description of the flaw.
- Required Evidence: Customers must upload two clear photographs: (1) an overall photo of the garment showing the brand label/tag, and (2) a high-resolution close-up photo clearly highlighting the specific stitch failure, zipper defect, or fabric flaw.
- Staff Review SLA: Because warranty evaluations require human technical appraisal, all warranty requests are queued for staff review. Our Quality Assurance specialists review the photographic evidence within 1 to 2 business days.
- Resolution and Dispatch: Upon staff approval, a reverse pickup order is generated. The replacement garment is released for packing and express dispatch as soon as the carrier registers the return package pickup scan.
