# ThreadNest Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

## 1. Returns and Pickups FAQs
- Q: What is ThreadNest's return window and return policy?
  A: ThreadNest provides a 30-day return window from the date of recorded delivery (`delivered_at`). Per system policy rule REFUND_POLICY_001, return requests are accepted only for orders in 'delivered' status. Per rule REFUND_POLICY_002, requests submitted after 30 days are not accepted. Items must be unworn, unwashed, unaltered, and have all original brand tags and packaging intact.
- Q: How do I schedule a return pickup?
  A: You can initiate a return directly through Tara, our AI Assistant, or via your account dashboard. Our courier partners (Delhivery, BlueDart, FedEx) will execute a doorstep pickup within 24 to 48 hours.
- Q: Do I need to pay for return shipping?
  A: No, reverse pickup is 100% free across India. If you reside in an unserviceable pin code and self-ship, ThreadNest reimburses up to ₹150 INR upon receipt proof.
- Q: What categories are non-returnable?
  A: Innerwear, lingerie, swimwear, socks, fabric face masks, customized garments, and clearance items marked 'Final Sale' cannot be returned due to hygiene regulations.

## 2. Size Exchanges and Replacements FAQs
- Q: Can I exchange an item for a different size?
  A: Yes. If a garment does not fit perfectly, you can request a size exchange through Tara or our support portal. You must specify the order ID, item name, and preferred size.
- Q: How does the replacement process work?
  A: Replacement requests for damaged, defective, or incorrectly sized items are submitted to our staff review queue for quick verification. Once approved (within 1 business day), a reverse pickup of the existing item is scheduled, and a replacement order is dispatched upon pickup confirmation.
- Q: Can I exchange an item for a different color or completely different product?
  A: Direct exchanges are permitted only for size variations of the same product style. If you wish to choose a different color or product, please return the delivered item for a refund or store credit and place a new order.
- Q: What if my required exchange size is out of stock?
  A: If your preferred size is out of stock, our team will issue a 100% refund or store credit so you can choose another style.

## 3. Shipping and Order Tracking FAQs
- Q: How much does shipping cost on ThreadNest?
  A: Standard shipping is 100% free on all orders of ₹999 INR and above. For orders under ₹999, a flat shipping fee of ₹99 INR is charged. Express priority delivery is available for select metro areas at ₹149 INR.
- Q: How long does delivery take?
  A: Delivery to metro cities takes 3 business days (1 to 2 business days for Express). Tier-2 cities take 3 to 5 business days, while remote regions and Northeast states take 5 to 7 business days. Orders placed before 2:00 PM IST on weekdays are dispatched within 24 hours.
- Q: Which couriers do you use, and how can I track my shipment?
  A: We partner with BlueDart, Delhivery, and FedEx India. You can track your parcel in real-time by asking Tara ('Where is my order?') or by using the tracking link sent via SMS and WhatsApp.
- Q: What happens if I miss my delivery?
  A: Our courier partners make up to 3 delivery attempts. The driver will call your registered phone number before visiting. If all 3 attempts fail, the parcel is returned to origin (RTO).

## 4. Payment Options and Billing FAQs
- Q: What payment methods are accepted on ThreadNest?
  A: We accept all major Indian and international payment options: UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), Credit and Debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay, American Express), Net Banking across 50+ banks, ThreadNest Store Credit/Wallet, and Cash on Delivery (COD).
- Q: Is Cash on Delivery (COD) available for all pin codes and order amounts?
  A: COD is available across 18,000+ pin codes for order values between ₹499 INR and ₹5,000 INR. Orders above ₹5,000 must be prepaid online. Handover OTP and exact cash/UPI scan at doorstep are required. Open-box inspection before payment is not allowed by courier rules.
- Q: What should I do if money was deducted from my account but the order failed?
  A: Payment gateways automatically reconcile and reverse failed transactions within 24 to 48 hours. If the funds do not reflect within 48 hours, contact support with your bank transaction reference (UTR/RRN) for prompt verification.

## 5. Refund Timelines and Auto-Approval FAQs
- Q: How does ThreadNest's refund approval process work?
  A: ThreadNest uses an automated two-tier approval workflow. Refund requests of ₹1,000 INR or less are auto-approved instantly by our system. Refund requests exceeding ₹1,000 INR are routed to our staff review queue and evaluated within 1 to 2 business days.
- Q: What is the maximum refund limit?
  A: In accordance with rule REFUND_POLICY_003, the maximum refund cannot exceed the total order value paid by the customer for that order.
- Q: When will I receive my refund money?
  A: For prepaid orders (cards, net banking), refunds credit within 5 to 7 business days after approval. UPI refunds credit within 24 to 48 hours. For COD orders, you will receive a secure link to enter your UPI ID or bank details (credited in 3-5 days), or you can choose instant ThreadNest Store Credit.

## 6. Order Cancellation and Address Changes FAQs
- Q: Can I cancel my order after placing it?
  A: Yes, you can cancel an order free of charge at any time before it is dispatched (while in 'pending', 'confirmed', or 'processing' status). Once an order is 'dispatched', cancellation is blocked in the system; you may refuse delivery at the doorstep or return the package after receipt.
- Q: Can I change my delivery address after placing an order?
  A: Delivery address can be updated while the order is in 'pending', 'confirmed', or 'processing' status using Tara or your dashboard. Once the order reaches 'dispatched' status, address modifications are frozen and cannot be altered.
- Q: What happens if I refuse delivery at the doorstep?
  A: The parcel will be returned to our warehouse as a Return to Origin (RTO). Once received and verified at our facility, a full refund for prepaid orders will be processed back to your original payment method.

## 7. Warranty Claims and Customer Care FAQs
- Q: What is ThreadNest's 6-month quality guarantee?
  A: All ThreadNest garments are covered by a 6-month warranty against manufacturing defects, including stitching unraveling, broken zippers, seam tears, and excessive fabric pilling under normal use. Normal wear and tear, accidents, and improper laundering are excluded.
- Q: How do I submit a warranty claim?
  A: Contact Tara or create a support ticket with your order ID, a brief explanation, and two photos (one of the whole item with tag, one close-up of the defect). Our QA team reviews claims within 1 to 2 business days and provides a free replacement or store credit.
- Q: How do I contact customer support or escalate an issue?
  A: You can chat 24/7 with our AI Assistant Tara. If your query requires human assistance, Tara can immediately generate an escalation support ticket (create_ticket) for our support team, who will respond within 4 business hours.
