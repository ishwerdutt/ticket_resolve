# ThreadNest Shipping and Delivery Policy

## 1. Shipping Rates and Free Delivery Threshold
ThreadNest offers transparent and economical shipping rates across India. We believe in rewarding our valued customers with free shipping benefits whenever possible:
- Free Standard Shipping: All orders with a total cart value of ₹999 INR or greater qualify for 100% free standard delivery across all serviceable pin codes nationwide. The free shipping discount applies automatically at checkout without requiring any promotional promo codes.
- Flat Standard Shipping Fee: For orders below ₹999 INR, a nominal flat shipping fee of ₹99 INR is applied to cover carrier handling and packaging expenses.
- Express Priority Shipping: For urgent deliveries, ThreadNest provides an optional Express Priority shipping upgrade at a flat rate of ₹149 INR, available for select Tier-1 and metropolitan cities during checkout.

All quoted shipping charges are fully inclusive of Goods and Services Tax (GST). If an order contains multiple items that are dispatched in separate consignments from different fulfillment centers, customers are charged only once at checkout—no additional shipping fees are assessed for split shipments.

## 2. Pan-India Delivery Zones and Estimated Timelines
ThreadNest fulfills orders across more than 19,000 pin codes in India. Estimated delivery transit times depend on the delivery destination zone:
- Tier-1 Metro Cities: Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Pune, and Ahmedabad. Standard delivery timeline is 3 business days. Express priority orders arrive within 1 to 2 business days.
- Tier-2 and Major Regional Centers: State capitals, major industrial hubs, and tier-2 urban areas (e.g., Jaipur, Lucknow, Chandigarh, Kochi, Indore, Surat). Delivery timeline is 3 to 5 business days.
- Tier-3 and Remote Regional Areas: Rural districts, Northeast states (Assam, Meghalaya, etc.), Jammu & Kashmir, Ladakh, and island territories (Andaman & Nicobar, Lakshadweep). Delivery timeline is 5 to 7 business days.

Order Processing SLA: Orders placed before 2:00 PM IST from Monday through Saturday are packed and dispatched within 24 hours. Orders placed after 2:00 PM IST, on Sundays, or on national public holidays are processed and dispatched on the next business day.

## 3. Courier Logistics Partners and Shipment Tracking
ThreadNest partners exclusively with India's premier logistics carriers to ensure fast, secure, and reliable deliveries across all urban and rural destinations:
- Integrated Courier Partners: Our primary domestic shipping carriers are BlueDart Express, Delhivery Supply Chain, and FedEx India. Carrier selection is automated based on optimal route speed, pin code serviceability, weather advisories, and carrier transit performance.
- Tracking Generation: As soon as an order is packed, quality checked, and handed over at our central warehouse facility, a unique shipment tracking number (Air Waybill / AWB) is generated and linked to the customer order record.
- Live Tracking Updates: Customers receive automated tracking notifications with a clickable tracking URL via SMS, WhatsApp, and registered email address. Customers can also ask Tara, our AI Support Assistant, to check live order status using the track_shipment tool, which displays real-time carrier status, current transit location hub, and the expected delivery date.
- Transit Milestone Alerts: Automated milestone alerts are triggered at key transit checkpoints, including 'Package Dispatched', 'Arrived at Local Delivery Hub', and 'Out for Delivery with Courier Executive'.

## 4. Delivery Attempts, Doorstep OTP, and Return to Origin (RTO)
Our courier partners are committed to successful doorstep delivery and adhere to standard verification protocols:
- Delivery Attempts: Carriers make up to three (3) consecutive delivery attempts on separate business days before designating an undelivered parcel for Return to Origin (RTO).
- Driver Contact and Notifications: The delivery executive calls the registered customer phone number before arrival. For secure high-value shipments, a 4-digit One-Time Password (OTP) is sent via SMS to the customer's phone, which must be shared with the delivery executive to accept handover.
- Return to Origin (RTO): If delivery fails after three attempts due to customer unavailability, incorrect address, or refusal, the package is returned to our fulfillment warehouse.
- Reshipment vs Refund: Once an RTO consignment is received back at our facility, our support team contacts the customer. If the customer requests re-delivery, a reshipment fee of ₹99 may apply. For prepaid orders where the customer does not respond within 48 hours of RTO receipt, a refund is processed to the original payment source minus standard shipping deductions.

## 5. Cash on Delivery (COD) Guidelines and Inspection Rules
ThreadNest offers Cash on Delivery (COD) to enhance customer convenience across eligible regions:
- Pin Code Serviceability: COD is supported across over 18,000 pin codes for cart values ranging from ₹499 INR up to ₹5,000 INR. Orders exceeding ₹5,000 INR must be prepaid via digital payment options (UPI, Credit/Debit card, Net Banking) for security reasons.
- Order Verification: First-time COD orders or high-value orders may trigger an automated WhatsApp or SMS verification confirmation link prior to warehouse picking.
- Payment Collection at Doorstep: Customers must pay the exact invoice amount to the courier representative. Couriers accept cash and digital UPI payments via handheld QR code scanners. Delivery personnel do not carry substantial cash change.
- Strict Doorstep Inspection Policy: Per courier partner operating regulations, 'Open Delivery' or unpacking and trying on garments prior to making payment is strictly prohibited. Customers must pay and accept delivery first. If any garment is subsequently found to be incorrect, damaged, or poorly fitting, ThreadNest's 30-day return policy and free reverse pickup guarantee ensure complete customer protection.
