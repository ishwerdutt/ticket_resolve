# ThreadNest Return and Refund Policy

## 1. Return Window and Eligibility (REFUND_POLICY_001 & REFUND_POLICY_002)
ThreadNest offers a customer-friendly 30-day return policy for all eligible apparel purchases. Customers may initiate a return request within exactly 30 calendar days from the recorded delivery timestamp. In accordance with system policy rule REFUND_POLICY_001, return and refund requests can only be initiated for orders that have reached the 'delivered' status. Orders that are currently in 'pending', 'confirmed', 'processing', or 'dispatched' statuses cannot be processed for a return; customers must await package delivery or follow our cancellation guidelines.

Furthermore, per policy rule REFUND_POLICY_002, the return window permanently expires once 30 days have elapsed following delivery. Return requests submitted on day 31 or later are automatically rejected by our automated system and customer support representatives. To qualify for a return and refund, apparel items must be returned in their original condition: unworn, unwashed, unaltered, and free from any perfumes, body odors, makeup marks, or pet hair. All original brand tags, price tags, barcodes, security loops, and original protective polybags must remain attached and intact. Any garment showing signs of laundering, fragrance, fabric strain, or detached tags will fail inspection and be returned to the customer at their expense.

## 2. Non-Returnable Items and Category Exclusions
To maintain strict hygiene, sanitary, and quality standards, certain product categories sold on ThreadNest are strictly non-returnable and non-exchangeable once delivered. Non-returnable merchandise includes:
1. Innerwear, lingerie, briefs, trunks, brassieres, shapewear, and thermal undergarments.
2. Swimwear and swim accessories due to direct body contact regulations.
3. Socks, stockings, and fabric face masks.
4. Custom-tailored, personalized, or altered garments (such as hemmed pants or custom embroidery).
5. Items marked explicitly as 'Final Sale' or 'Clearance' on the product page and checkout invoice.
6. ThreadNest digital gift cards, promotional e-vouchers, and store credit balances.

If a non-returnable item is received in a physically damaged or defective state, or if an incorrect item was shipped, customers are covered under our Damaged Goods guarantee and may request a replacement or refund within 48 hours of delivery. Attempting to return worn or ineligible non-returnable items via pickup will result in rejection by the logistics executive or our return fulfillment warehouse inspection team.

## 3. Reverse Pickup Logistics and Quality Inspection
ThreadNest provides 100% free reverse pickup services across all serviceable pin codes throughout India. Once a return request is approved in the system, our integrated logistics partners (Delhivery, BlueDart, or FedEx Express) will schedule a doorstep pickup within 24 to 48 business hours. Customers will receive an SMS and email notification with the pickup agent's tracking details and assigned courier partner.

At the time of pickup, the courier delivery executive will conduct a standard preliminary doorstep inspection to verify that the item matches the SKU on the return manifest and that original tags are present. Customers must pack the item securely in its original packaging or a tamper-evident envelope. In the rare event that a customer's pin code is located in an out-of-delivery area (ODA) where reverse pickup is unavailable, customers may self-ship the parcel via India Post Speed Post or any reputable courier service. Upon sharing a photograph of the courier receipt and tracking number with our support team, ThreadNest will reimburse courier shipping expenses up to ₹150 INR into the customer's wallet or original payment source.

## 4. Refund Approval Thresholds and Staff Review (REFUND_POLICY_003)
ThreadNest implements an intelligent two-tiered refund authorization workflow designed for rapid customer satisfaction and security compliance:
- Automated Instant Approval (<= ₹1,000 INR): Any eligible refund request where the refundable order total is less than or equal to ₹1,000 INR is auto-approved immediately by the system upon return request submission or warehouse receipt. No human agent intervention is required, ensuring lightning-fast resolution for smaller transactions.
- Human Staff Review (> ₹1,000 INR): Any refund request exceeding ₹1,000 INR (> ₹1000.00) is automatically routed to our specialized compliance and support staff queue for manual verification. Our review team evaluates the order history, delivery confirmation, and item condition within 1 to 2 business days before issuing formal approval.

In all instances, per rule REFUND_POLICY_003, the maximum refund amount authorized for an order cannot exceed the total amount actually paid by the customer for that order (including applicable discounts, coupon deductions, and taxes). Partial refunds are supported proportionally when returning individual items from multi-product orders.

## 5. Settlement Timelines and Payment Methods
Once a refund request has received automated approval or staff authorization, the disbursement timeline depends on the original payment method:
- Prepaid Orders (UPI, Credit Cards, Debit Cards, Net Banking): Refunds are processed through our payment gateway within 24 hours of approval. The funds will reflect in the customer's original funding bank account or credit card statement within 5 to 7 business days, subject to the issuing bank's clearing cycle. For instant UPI transactions (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), funds often reflect within 24 to 48 hours.
- Cash on Delivery (COD) Orders: For orders fulfilled via Cash on Delivery, customers will receive a secure SMS and email link to enter their preferred UPI ID or Bank Account Details (NEFT with IFSC code). Upon submission, funds are transferred within 3 to 5 business days. Alternatively, customers may select immediate ThreadNest Store Credit / Wallet balance, which is credited instantly and valid for 12 months.
- Shipping Fee Refunds: Original standard shipping charges (₹99 for orders below ₹999) are non-refundable unless the return is due to an error by ThreadNest (such as shipping a defective, damaged, or incorrect product).

## 6. Damaged, Defective, or Incorrect Items on Delivery
If you receive a ThreadNest package that is damaged, unsealed, or contains an incorrect style, size, or defective garment, please notify us within 48 hours of delivery. Customers can initiate a claim directly through our AI Assistant (Tara) or by opening a support ticket with high-resolution photographs illustrating the defect, packaging label, and attached tags.

Upon receipt of the claim, our priority resolution team will verify the report within 24 hours. For validated cases, customers are offered an immediate free replacement shipped via express courier or a 100% full refund to their original payment source, including any shipping fees paid. ThreadNest arranges a hassle-free reverse pickup of the defective unit at zero expense to the customer. Customers are strongly advised not to discard original shipping boxes, tags, or mailing flyers until the issue is completely resolved with customer support. In cases where the same item is out of stock, full store credit or direct bank refund is provided immediately.
